Meet the Baker

A Little Story Behind Every Slice

Nusrat Jahan Founder & Head Baker

Hi, I'm Nusrat! What started as a small weekend hobby in my home kitchen in 2018 has grown into Rabia’s Cake House — a little bakery built on big dreams and even bigger slices of cake.

I believe a cake is never just a dessert. It is the centrepiece of your celebration, the surprise on a child's face, and the sweet memory you keep for years. That is why I bake every single order myself, from whisking the batter to piping the final swirl of buttercream.

With 7+ years of experience and hundreds of custom designs delivered, my promise is simple: real ingredients, no shortcuts, and a cake that looks as good as it tastes.

  • 🥚 Fresh, premium ingredients
  • 🎨 Fully custom designs
  • 🚚 Safe & on-time delivery
  • 💗 Baked with real passion
